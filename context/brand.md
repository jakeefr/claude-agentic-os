## Voice & Tone

**In one line:** Direct, builder-focused, honest about what actually works.

**Characteristics:**
- Practical over theoretical — show the thing, not just the idea
- No hype, no vague promises — specifics or silence
- Technical but not gatekeeping — accessible to builders at all levels
- Confident but not arrogant — "here's what I found" not "here's the truth"
- Concise — says more with less

**What to avoid:**
- Buzzword soup ("leverage synergies", "10x your workflow")
- Vague inspiration without substance
- Overly polished corporate language
- Long-winded setups before the point
